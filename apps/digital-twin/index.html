      Calibration and verification

      Confirm chemical injection points, exact analyzer taps, doses as product or active ingredient, and historian clock. Match dose changes to downstream samples using travel time and sample-line lag. Fit the two-dose response using plant and jar-test data, then validate it on later runs, including different source-water conditions. Define approved dose ranges and performance constraints with operators before ranking cost and treatment outcomes.

      Nominal travel uses Plant Flow volumes with all units in service and equal flow splits through parallel basins and filters. It is a hydraulic screening estimate. The demo combines both entered doses on a common mass basis. EPA turbidity and treatment optimization guidance
