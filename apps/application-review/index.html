A Familiar Review, A Clearer Workspace

  1. Set Up The Review. Assign one to four reviewers, leave unused slots blank, and confirm points. Defaults are Yes = 4, Maybe = 1, No = 0.
  2. Add Applicants. Enter name, state, requested wage, and licenses or certifications. Each applicant receives a permanent ID; editing or sorting does not disconnect their reviews.
  3. Record Your Perspective. Select your reviewer name at the top of the page. Write job-related qualifications, strengths, concerns, or interview questions. Choose Yes to recommend an interview, Maybe for discussion, or No to decline.
  4. Leave Unfinished Votes Blank. Notes can be saved without a vote. A blank vote is incomplete, not a No.
  5. Compare Completed Reviews. Other reviewers' notes, votes, and scores appear after you save your own vote for that applicant. Rankings appear after you vote for every active applicant. Total points determine rank. Two Yes votes, one Maybe, and one No score 9 points with the defaults. Only compare final rankings once all assigned reviewers have voted.
  6. Keep A Record. Print the full applicant summary or download a complete backup, including notes and votes. Archived applicants remain available in Review Setup. Archive an entire hiring round in Hiring Reviews; Show Archived Reviews lets you open or restore it.

Reviewer selection identifies whose perspective you are recording; it is not a login or a private review mode. Saved notes and votes are shared.
