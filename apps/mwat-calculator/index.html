Stream Maximum Weekly Average Temperature (MWAT) Calculator

Upload two overlapping CSV files containing 15-minute stream temperature readings to calculate the Maximum Weekly Average Temperature (MWAT) for a selected month.

Each CSV should contain a single header row. The timestamp must be located in column B and the corresponding temperature in column C. All other columns will be ignored.

Inputs

The MWAT is calculated by averaging all temperature data points collected during a calendar day and then averaging those daily averages across seven consecutive days. For windows that span months, the result is attributed to the month that contains at least four of the seven days, as shown in the MWAT guidance chart.
