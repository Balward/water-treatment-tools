Distribution Analysis

ℹ️ How to use: Select a variable to see its frequency distribution. Adjust bin count to change histogram detail. Statistics panel shows key metrics like mean, median, and standard deviation.

Key Statistics Explained:
• STD Deviation: Measures how spread out your data is from the average. Lower values mean data points are clustered close to the mean; higher values indicate more variation.
• Q1 (First Quartile): The value below which 25% of your data falls. Useful for identifying the lower range of typical values.
• Q3 (Third Quartile): The value below which 75% of your data falls. Helps identify the upper range of typical values. The difference between Q3 and Q1 shows the middle 50% spread of your data.
