How The Estimate Works

Suggested PAR = average daily net stock decline × (lead time + review interval) × safety buffer factor, rounded up. It uses flat and declining intervals of 45 days or less. Increasing intervals are excluded because they may contain restocking. At least three eligible intervals, 21 eligible days, 28 days of history, and some decline are required.

Counts are not a usage ledger. Restocking between counts, corrections, and stockouts can hide demand. Treat this as a starting point and account for critical spares, shelf life, and unusual use. A flat count does not prove an item is unnecessary. On duplicate dates, the most recently updated compatible completed inventory is used.
