When Would Chemicals Appear Here?

Completing a chemical inventory would add a recommendation when its reorder level is reached, or when its forecast says the order is due with seven days of delivery notice. A chemical would have one open recommendation that updates with new readings. Placing an order would move it to Placed Orders.

Draft readings would stay out of ordering decisions. A placed order would be shown with the next recommendation so operators can check deliveries already arranged.
