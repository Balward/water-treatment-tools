How To Use This Tool

Quick Start Checklist

Use presets as a starting point, then enter your measured conditions.

  1. Seasonal preset: Choose the profile that best reflects today’s source water conditions.
  2. Input parameters: Enter raw water quality data from the most recent grab or online instruments.
  3. Request prediction: Select Predict Dose to calculate an estimate from your inputs.
  4. Apply & verify: Review the estimate against plant procedures and measured response.
  5. Document: Save a copy of your inputs and estimate for later review.
