THE MATH

How This Is Calculated

Available chlorine is expressed as an equivalent mass of Cl₂. Solution weight includes the whole liquid product.

Chlorine required (g) = Addition (mg/L) × Basin volume (L) ÷ 1,000
Solution volume (L) = Chlorine required (g) ÷ Available chlorine in product (g/L)

  3. Convert Product Strength

    NaOCl weight % uses the Cl₂/NaOCl molar-mass ratio. Available-chlorine weight % does not need that factor. Trade % × 10 gives g/L.

  4. Calculate Solution Amount

    Convert liters to US gallons, mL, fluid ounces, and solution weight.

Calculations use 3.785411784 L/US gal, 128 US fl oz/US gal, and 453.59237 g/lb. Specific gravity is treated numerically as kg/L using water density ≈ 1 kg/L. The calculation neglects the volume of the added solution. Results are rounded only for display.
