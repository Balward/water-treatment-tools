# SOP writing conventions

Use clear imperatives: "Verify", "Open", "Record", "Notify", "Stop". Keep actions in their operational sequence. Use consistent terminology, equipment labels, capitalization, and units based on source. State prerequisites before dependent steps without silently moving source steps. Preserve conditional logic (if/then), stop-work conditions, acceptance thresholds, and PPE. Keep Purpose concise, Background contextual, Notes for operational cautions, Safety for hazards/controls, Equipment for required items, and procedure for ordered actions. Never invent a chemical concentration, pressure rating, valve lineup, drainage pathway, disposal method, calibration criterion, or compliance claim. Flag missing information and ask for a technical decision. Do not treat style preference as authority to alter technical meaning.
